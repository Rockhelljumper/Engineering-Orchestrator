Mission Control visual direction: “Telemetry Console”

A flight-control console crossed with an engineering drawing: graphite or drafting-white panels on a faint blueprint grid, drawing-style title strips, annunciator lamps for state and monospaced tabular numerals. The six signal colours are the only saturated colours on screen, so colour always means a state, and the trust level (verified, recorded, unverified) changes the shape of the mark as well as its colour.

Normative spec: proposal.md §4.2. Fonts: Archivo and IBM Plex Mono (Google Fonts here; bundled in production).
